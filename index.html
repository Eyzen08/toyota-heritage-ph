The Philosophy

More than a car.
A legacy.

At the heart of Toyota beats Kaizen — the philosophy of continuous improvement. It is the belief that nothing is ever finished, that every process, every part, every journey can be made better. This spirit, born in a small Japanese loom workshop, now drives millions of Filipinos to work, to family, and to adventure every single day.
